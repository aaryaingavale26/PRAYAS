Verbatim Presentation Script (180 Seconds)

0:00 – 0:35 • The Problem & Vision
1. Digital Inaccessibility in Hiring

"Over 1.3 billion people live with disabilities worldwide, yet over 95% of job application portals fail basic accessibility audits. Missing labels, broken tab sequences, and intimidating essay questions force qualified candidates with motor, visual, or cognitive disabilities to abandon their applications.

PRAYAS 3.0 is an AI-powered accessibility equalizer. It doesn't ask companies to rebuild their websites; instead, it runs directly over existing portals, giving candidates full voice control, verified passport autofill, and assistive AI draft generation grounded strictly in their actual resume."

0:35 – 1:15 • Live Demo: Navigation & Autofill
2. Voice Navigation & Passport Autofill

"On our live demo portal, notice Field 3 lacks an accessible label, and Field 4 has a broken keyboard tab trap. When the candidate speaks 'Next field', PRAYAS navigates semantically, bypassing the broken tab order. When they say 'Read question', PRAYAS extracts the element's metadata and reads it aloud.

With a single command—'Fill my details'—PRAYAS populates Name, Email, Phone, and Experience directly from their verified Accessibility Passport, saving exhaustive repetitive typing."

🎬 Live Action: Click "Simulate Autofill" or say "Fill my details" on demo form.
1:15 – 2:05 • Live Demo: Conversational RAG
3. AI Conversational Agent & RAG Grounding

"For the behavioral essay question in Field 5, PRAYAS initiates a respectful 7-step dialog. It reads the prompt, asks candidate consent, and connects to our FastAPI backend to generate a personalized draft backed by vector citations from their uploaded resume.

Crucially, PRAYAS never hallucinates experiences. If a topic is absent from the resume, it pauses and prompts the user for clarification. The draft is read aloud, and only inserted into the field once the candidate explicitly approves it."

🎬 Live Action: Press Alt+H on Field 5 → Consent Yes → Review draft citations → Accept.
2:05 – 2:35 • Architecture & Safety
4. Architecture & Safeguards

"Our system is built on two strict safety principles:
1. Zero Auto-Submission Guarantee: PRAYAS will never automatically submit an application. The candidate retains final human agency to review and click submit.
2. 100% Keyboard Parity: Every voice command has an instantaneous keyboard equivalent (Alt+M, Alt+N, Alt+F, Alt+H) alongside a manual text input for non-verbal users."

2:35 – 3:00 • Impact & Limitations
5. Impact, Limitations & Roadmap

"Current Limitations: This 36-hour prototype utilizes Chromium's Web Speech API, which requires an active internet connection. In our production roadmap, we are integrating local Whisper on-device models for privacy in air-gapped environments and expanding to regional multi-lingual support.

PRAYAS 3.0 returns dignity, speed, and autonomy to candidates with disabilities. Thank you!"
